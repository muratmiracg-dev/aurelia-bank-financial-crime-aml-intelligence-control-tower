# Ethics and human oversight

Financial-crime analytics operates on incomplete evidence and can materially affect people. This project therefore uses the following non-negotiable controls:

- alerts describe behaviour, never criminality;
- all data are synthetic and clearly labelled;
- every case requires trained human review;
- no report, restriction or adverse decision is automated;
- thresholds are internal parameters and cannot replace legal judgment;
- network and anomaly features are supporting signals, not stand-alone grounds;
- legitimate explanations and contradictory evidence must be documented;
- validation labels are isolated from detection;
- current sanctions and risk classifications must come from approved production sources;
- model performance must be tested across customer segments and potential proxy variables before use.

The appropriate production target is a controlled decision-support workflow with traceable evidence, challenge and accountability—not autonomous enforcement.
